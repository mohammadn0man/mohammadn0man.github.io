About the App

Munajaat Nomani is a simple mobile application designed to help Muslims incorporate daily supplications into their routine.

The app organizes duas by days of the week, making it easy to develop a consistent habit of remembrance and reflection.

With beautiful Arabic text, Urdu translations, and a distraction-free interface, the app provides a peaceful experience for reading daily supplications.
